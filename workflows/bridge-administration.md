# Bridge administration

## Programme setup

Confirm the organisation, participating vendor or vendors, eligible employees, meal dates, cut-offs, subsidy or contribution rules and enabled notifications.

## Publishing a menu

1. Prepare the menu in the correct vendor and programme context.
2. Confirm dates, prices and availability.
3. Publish once the menu is ready for employees.
4. Review the employee-facing view.
5. Confirm that reminders are enabled only where the organisation has approved them.

## Collection attribution

Where deferred identification is enabled:

1. capture the configured collection identifier;
2. serve the order using the operational queue;
3. review suggested matches in the authorised admin view;
4. confirm unique matches or manually review shared and unmatched identifiers;
5. complete attribution before the configured final state.

Do not expose unrestricted employee search on a public kiosk. Collect only the identifier required by the programme.

## Administration and review

Use the admin workspace for multi-vendor visibility, issue resolution, settlement review and exports. Keep programme decisions and exceptions traceable.
