# Menus and publication

A menu is useful only when it is published to the correct audience, site and service window.

## Before publishing

- Confirm the vendor, site, Bridge programme or Gather event.
- Check item names, options, prices and tax treatment.
- Check dates, cut-offs and service windows.
- Remove or disable unavailable items.
- Confirm the intended ordering channels.

## Publish and verify

1. Publish from the authorised VendorOS context.
2. Open the customer or employee experience.
3. Confirm that the visible menu, price and availability match the source.
4. If reminders are enabled, confirm the audience and timing before sending.

Do not create separate competing menus to correct a channel mismatch. Record the affected context and escalate the publication issue.
