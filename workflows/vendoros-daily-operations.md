# VendorOS daily operations

## Start of service

1. Sign in with your own staff account.
2. Confirm the active vendor and site.
3. Confirm that today's menu is published to the intended channel or programme.
4. Check item availability, service windows and any capacity controls.
5. Confirm that the KDS or order queue is connected and showing the correct site.

## During service

1. Review new orders and their channel or programme context.
2. Lock or accept work using the available action.
3. Prepare from the ticket details; do not infer missing options.
4. Mark the order served when handed over at the venue.
5. Mark it delivered when the configured journey requires final delivery or attribution.
6. Escalate mismatched payment or customer details instead of editing financial records casually.

## End of service

1. Confirm that no valid order remains unintentionally pending.
2. Record cancelled, refunded or exceptional orders.
3. Export the reporting period needed for reconciliation.
4. Note operational issues for the next shift.

Kitchen devices should remain focused on fulfilment. Reporting, reconciliation and account administration belong in the authorised admin workspace.
