# Patatte Help and Product Guide

Patatte provides the structure behind great dining experiences. This guide explains how the products fit together, how to complete common tasks, and what to check when something does not work as expected.

## Products

- [VendorOS](product/vendoros.md) helps food operators receive, prepare, fulfil and understand orders.
- [GO](product/go.md) provides a customer storefront, self-service kiosk and staff POS.
- [Bridge](product/bridge.md) coordinates workplace meal programmes.
- [Gather](product/gather.md) supports temporary event and group-ordering experiences.

## How to use this guide

Begin with the product page for the task you are trying to complete. If the task is failing, follow the matching troubleshooting guide before escalating. The escalation guide lists the evidence that helps support resolve a problem quickly.

Features can vary by deployment and account configuration. When the application and this guide differ, do not repeatedly retry a payment or order. Record the evidence listed in the escalation guide and contact your Patatte support channel.
