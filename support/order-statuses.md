# Order statuses

Status labels describe where an order is in its fulfilment journey. The wording shown to a customer can vary by product or catalogue type, but the operational meaning should remain consistent.

| Operational state | Meaning | Customer-safe interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pending | The order was received but has not reached its preparation lock or commitment point | Order received |
| Locked | The order is committed to the fulfilment window and can be prepared | Confirmed or being prepared |
| Served | The food or item has been handed over at the service point | Ready, served or collected |
| Delivered | Delivery or final fulfilment has been confirmed | Completed or delivered |

## When a status looks wrong

- Confirm you are viewing the correct order number.
- Confirm the vendor and site.
- Refresh once and note the time.
- Ask the fulfilment team whether the physical handover occurred.
- Do not force a later status merely to remove a ticket.
- Escalate when the application and physical fulfilment disagree.

Merchandise can use labels such as Processing, Ready for collection and Collected while still mapping to the appropriate operational state.
