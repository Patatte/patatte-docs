# Gather troubleshooting

## Event link does not open

Confirm the full link, event name and service window. The link may be expired, unpublished or intended for another service window.

## Menu is empty

Confirm the event is active, the vendor is published for the window and products remain available. Record the event, vendor and time.

## Vendor invitation fails

Confirm the complete invitation link and whether it was already accepted. Do not forward a private invitation broadly. Ask the organiser to issue or confirm the appropriate invitation.

## Attendee cannot track an order

Use the exact order reference from confirmation. Confirm the event and service window. If payment completed without an order reference, escalate with the payment reference and time instead of placing another order.
