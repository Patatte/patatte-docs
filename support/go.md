# GO troubleshooting

## Storefront or kiosk shows the wrong items

- Confirm the vendor and site shown on screen.
- Refresh once.
- Confirm the service window is open.
- Ask the vendor whether the product is published and available.
- Record the product name, channel and time.

## POS access is denied

- Confirm the account is a cashier, Site Chief or vendor administrator.
- Kitchen staff should use VendorOS KDS.
- Confirm the user is assigned to the active site.
- If no register is available, ask the Site Chief to review the active shift or register assignment.

## An item will not add to the cart

Confirm it is not sold out and complete all required options. Close and reopen the item once. If it still fails, capture the product name and visible validation message.

## Payment or submission is slow

Do not click the final action repeatedly. Wait for a result, check for an order number and follow the payment provider's confirmation before retrying. Escalate with the time, amount and payment reference, excluding private card details.

## The kiosk contains another guest's order

Cancel or start over before entering any customer details. Ask staff for help if the kiosk cannot return to the welcome screen.
