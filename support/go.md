# GO troubleshooting

## The storefront or kiosk shows the wrong items

- Confirm the vendor or venue shown on screen.
- Refresh once.
- Confirm the service window is open.
- Ask the vendor whether the item is available.
- Record the product name, ordering channel and time.

## An item will not add to the cart

Confirm it is not sold out and complete all required options. Close and reopen the item once. If it still fails, capture the product name and visible validation message.

## Payment or submission is slow

Do not click the final action repeatedly. Wait for a result, check for an order number and follow the payment provider's confirmation before retrying. Escalate with the time, amount and safe payment reference. Never include full card or mobile-money credentials.

## The kiosk contains another guest's order

Cancel or start over before entering any personal details. Ask a member of staff for help if the kiosk cannot return to the welcome screen.
