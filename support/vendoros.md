# VendorOS troubleshooting

## An order is missing from the queue

1. Confirm the customer received an order number.
2. Confirm the order belongs to this vendor and site.
3. Confirm you are viewing the correct status tab and service window.
4. Check whether another approved staff member can see the order.
5. Record the channel: storefront, kiosk, POS, Bridge or Gather.
6. Escalate without asking the customer to submit again.

## A staff member sees the wrong site or navigation

1. Confirm the staff account and role.
2. Sign out and sign in again.
3. Confirm the assigned site with a vendor administrator.
4. Kitchen staff should use the KDS order workspace, not POS.
5. Do not share an administrator account to bypass access.

## A product is unavailable unexpectedly

Check whether the catalogue is published, the service window is open, stock is available and site capacity has not been reached. Record the product and active site before changing anything.

## No sound or message was received

Confirm the order is visible in the queue first. Check device volume, browser notification permission, connectivity and the configured contact. Notification behaviour can vary by deployment; the queue remains the source for operational action.
