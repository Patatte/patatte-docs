# Troubleshooting

Use this section before escalating. First determine whether the issue concerns access, configuration, menu publication, availability, order submission, payment, fulfilment, reporting or notification.

## First response

1. Stop repeated order submissions or payments.
2. Record the product, account, site and ordering channel.
3. Capture the order, transaction, programme or event reference.
4. Record the exact time, time zone and visible status.
5. Check connectivity and refresh once.
6. Confirm the signed-in role and active site.
7. Follow the relevant product guide.
8. If unresolved, use the [support escalation checklist](escalation.md).

## Safety rules

- Do not submit an order again until the first attempt has been checked.
- Do not repeat a payment because confirmation is delayed.
- Do not change stock, capacity or access merely to hide a symptom.
- Do not edit or delete financial records to force a reconciliation match.
- Never share passwords, full payment credentials or unredacted customer data.
