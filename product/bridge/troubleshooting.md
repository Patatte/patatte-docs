# Bridge troubleshooting

## No programme or meal is visible

Confirm your workplace account, meal date and ordering window. Your programme administrator can check eligibility and programme setup.

## The order result is unclear

Check your order history or status before trying again. Repeated submission can create confusion.

## The contribution looks wrong

Do not submit until the displayed contribution has been checked with your programme administrator.

## Collection cannot find the order

Confirm the date, location and order reference. Do not share another employee's personal details.

For unresolved issues, [prepare a useful support request](../../support/escalation.md).
