# Welcome to Bridge

Bridge helps you take part in your organisation's workplace meal programme.

With Bridge, you can see the meals and dates available to you, review any contribution shown, place an order before the cut-off and check its status.

## Start here

1. [Access your workplace account](access.md).
2. [View available meals](available-meals.md).
3. [Place an order](place-an-order.md).
4. [Track and collect your order](track-and-collect.md).

Features and programme rules can vary by organisation. Always follow the dates, contribution and collection instructions shown in your account.
