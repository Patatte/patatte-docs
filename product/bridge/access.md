# Access your workplace account

Use the access method provided by your organisation. Keep sign-in and verification messages private, and do not use another employee's account.

After signing in, confirm that the correct workplace programme appears before ordering.

## If you cannot access Bridge

- Check that you used the approved workplace details.
- Confirm that your organisation has added you to the programme.
- Retry once in a supported browser.
- If access still fails, contact your workplace programme administrator or approved Patatte support channel.

Never send a password or one-time verification code in a support request.
