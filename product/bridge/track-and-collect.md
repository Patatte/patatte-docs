# Track and collect your order

Use Bridge to check the current status of your order. Statuses reflect where the order is in the programme and fulfilment flow.

At collection, use the reference or identification method shown for your programme. Keep your workplace and order details private, and never collect using another employee's identity.

If the meal cannot be found, keep your order reference and ask the collection team or programme administrator to check the correct date and location.

See [Order statuses](../../support/order-statuses.md) for general status guidance.
