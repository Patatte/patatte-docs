# Bridge

Bridge helps organisations coordinate workplace meal programmes with less daily follow-through.

## Employee experience

Employees see the menus, dates and contribution rules available to them, then submit their order before the programme cut-off. Published-menu reminders may be sent using the contact details approved for the programme.

Availability, contribution and ordering windows can vary by organisation. Confirm the programme shown before placing an order.

## Collection

Use the collection reference or identification method shown for your programme. Do not share another employee's details or use an unrelated workplace account.
