# Roles and sites

A site is a fulfilment-capable operating location, such as a restaurant branch, main kitchen or remote cafeteria. Site access prevents one location's live operations from being mixed with another's.

## Common roles

| Role | Typical scope | Typical access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vendor Super Admin | Highest vendor-level scope | Vendor-wide administration and exceptional controls |
| Vendor Admin | All sites for one vendor | Cross-site configuration, people and reporting |
| Site Chief | One assigned site | Day-to-day site operations and supervision |
| Cashier | One assigned site | POS register and order capture |
| Kitchen staff | One assigned site | Food preparation and fulfilment queue |

A user can only work in a site they have been assigned. If the correct site is missing, a vendor administrator must review the assignment.

## Institution and site are different

An institution identifies a customer or service relationship. A site identifies where fulfilment happens. A vendor can serve many institutions from one kitchen, and a remote cafeteria can be its own site when it can fulfil orders locally.

## Safe access checks

- Confirm the signed-in account.
- Confirm the vendor.
- Confirm the active site before starting a shift or preparing an order.
- Do not share accounts between sites or roles.
- Ask a vendor administrator to correct assignments instead of selecting an unrelated site.
