# Multi-site operations

Multi-site accounts separate business-wide administration from day-to-day site work.

- Vendor administrators can review the authorised business context across sites.
- Site-scoped staff should see and act only on their assigned site.
- Menus, availability, capacity, devices and reporting filters must use the correct site.
- A kiosk, POS or KDS should not be moved between sites without updating and verifying its assignment.

Before comparing performance across sites, confirm that the same date range, status definition and payment treatment are being used.

If a user can see the wrong site or cannot see an assigned site, treat it as an access-scope issue and escalate without sharing credentials.
