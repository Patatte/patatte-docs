# Glossary

Catalogue
: The collection of products a vendor or organisation can publish.

Channel
: A place where an order is captured, such as a storefront, kiosk, POS, Bridge or Gather.

Context
: The commercial or service setting in which a catalogue or order is used.

Fulfilment
: Preparing, handing over, collecting or delivering an order.

Gather
: Patatte's temporary event and group-ordering experience.

GO
: Patatte's live-venue storefront, kiosk and POS experience.

Institution
: An organisation served by a vendor. It is not automatically a fulfilment site.

KDS
: Kitchen display system used to prepare and fulfil food orders.

Parent context
: A vendor-level grouping for related commercial or service contexts.

Publication
: Making a catalogue available to an approved context and its channels.

Site
: An operationally closed, fulfilment-capable location such as a branch, kitchen or cafeteria.

Site Chief
: A site-scoped operational supervisor.

VendorOS
: Patatte's vendor administration and operational fulfilment system.
